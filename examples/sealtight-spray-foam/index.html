Why spray foam?

40% of your home’s energy leaks out through air exchange.

Heating and cooling account for about 56% of the energy use in a typical home, and up to 40% of a home’s energy is lost through air exchange. Closed-cell spray foam fills every gap for a complete seal, with an aged R-value of 6.3 per inch – and it meets the new building standards.
