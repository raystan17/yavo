The problem

A weak site quietly costs you calls

Most customers find a local business on their phone. If the site is hard to use on a phone, the job goes to the next name on Google.

Built for a big screen, not a phone

In our first batch of checkups we found homepages that render about 1,000 pixels wide on a phone, so visitors have to pinch and zoom before they can read anything.

The phone number does not call

Numbers shown as plain text, contact by email link only, and no quote form. Every extra step loses people who were ready to book.

Old, unfinished, or missing

Template text left in, a 2016 copyright in the footer, lorem ipsum in the reviews, or no website at all. Customers notice, even when owners do not.

Google does not know you

An incomplete Google Business Profile, few reviews, and no recent photos means competitors show up first for the same search.

Straight answers

Questions owners ask us

How much will this cost me, in total?

Website builds start at $1,500 CAD. Care is $79 CAD per month and Growth is $199 CAD per month. Online booking and job tracking are quoted after we see how your business runs. Your exact price is confirmed in writing before work starts.

Can I just build it myself with a website builder?

You can, and for some owners that works. The cost is your time: writing pages, fixing the phone layout, setting up Google, and keeping it updated while you run jobs. Start with the free checkup either way. Many of the top fixes are free to do yourself, and we will point them out honestly.

How long does it take?

Your timeline is agreed in writing before we start, so you know the launch target before you commit. Send photos and wording early and there is less waiting on both sides.

What happens if I leave?

You keep your domain, your website and your photos. They are your business assets. We will hand over what you need so another provider can pick things up.

I am not in Brandon. Can you still help?

Yes. The free checkup is online and available to local service businesses anywhere in Canada. Brandon is where we started and where our first client is. It is not a limit on who we serve.

I already have a website. Is the checkup still useful?

Yes. Most checkups are for owners who already have a site. We look at how it works on a phone, how a customer calls or books, and where leads are being lost.

Can I talk to a person before I decide anything?

Yes. Call or text 204-901-2941, or send the checkup request with your phone number and ask for a call in the business details. We will call you back to walk through the findings. The checkup itself is free either way.
