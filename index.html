Why the phone stays quiet

Most customers find you on a phone. Most sites were never built for one.

We ran checkups on dozens of local service sites. The same three problems kept costing owners jobs they never knew they lost.

PROBLEM 01

Built for a desk, read on a phone

Pages render a thousand pixels wide, so visitors pinch and zoom before they can read a word. Most leave instead.

We build phone first. Every page is easy to read with one thumb.
PROBLEM 02

The number does not call

Phone numbers shown as plain text, contact by email only, no quote form. Every extra step loses people who were ready to book.

Tap to call and a 60-second quote form on every screen.

An incomplete Google Business Profile, few reviews and no recent photos means the competitor down the road shows up first.

We clean up your profile and keep reviews and photos coming in.

Straight answers

Questions owners ask us

How much will this cost me, in total?

Website builds start at $1,500 CAD. Care is $79 CAD per month and Growth is $199 CAD per month. Online booking and job tracking are quoted after we see how your business runs. Your exact price is confirmed in writing before work starts.

Can I just build it myself with a website builder?

You can, and for some owners that works. The cost is your time: writing pages, fixing the phone layout, setting up Google, and keeping it updated while you run jobs. Start with the free checkup either way. Many of the top fixes are free to do yourself and we will point them out honestly.

Can I really see the site before I pay?

Yes. We build your site and send you the link. You check the wording, photos, services and prices. Only when you are happy does the build get paid for and launched on your domain.

What happens if I leave?

You keep your domain, your website and your photos. They are your business assets. We hand over what the next provider needs so nothing is held hostage.

I am not in Manitoba. Can you still help?

Yes. The checkup is online and the whole process works by phone, text and email, so we work with local service businesses anywhere in Canada. Brandon is where we started and where our first client is. It is not a limit on who we serve.

I already have a website. Is the checkup still useful?

Most checkups are for owners who already have a site. We look at how it works on a phone, how a customer calls or books, and where leads are being lost. Often the fixes are small.

Can I talk to a person before I decide anything?

Yes. Call or text 204-901-2941, or send the checkup request and ask for a call in the details box. We will walk through the findings with you. The checkup is free either way.
