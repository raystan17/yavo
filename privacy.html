Privacy policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Who we are

YAVO builds and looks after websites and Google Business Profiles for local service businesses. We are based in Brandon, Manitoba and serve businesses across Canada. Contact for privacy questions: YAVO at colesales@proton.me or 204-901-2941.

What we collect

When you send the free checkup or quote form, we collect the name, phone number, email address, business name, city and province, website address and message you provide. Our hosting and analytics tools may also record basic technical information such as page views and button clicks.

Why we collect it

We use this information only to prepare and send your checkup, reply to your request, provide a quote if you ask for one, and keep records of work we have done for you. We do not sell personal information.

Consent

The form asks for your clear consent before anything is sent. You can withdraw consent at any time by contacting us. Withdrawing consent will not affect work already completed.

Who can see it

Form messages are delivered through FormSubmit to our business inbox. Website hosting is provided through Vercel. We share personal information only with the service providers needed to run the website and deliver your request, or when required by law.

How long we keep it

Checkup and quote requests are kept only as long as needed to follow up and keep business records, then deleted. Client records are kept for the period needed to provide the service and meet legal requirements.

Your choices

You can ask what personal information we hold about you, ask for corrections, or ask for deletion, subject to legal requirements. Contact us using the details above and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Security

We use reputable providers, account passwords and access limits to protect information. No online system is perfectly secure, so please do not send passwords, payment card numbers or other sensitive documents through the website form.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a material way, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
