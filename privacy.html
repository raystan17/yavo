Privacy policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026. Applies to yavo-web.vercel.app and, once connected, yavo.ca.

The short version. We only collect what you type into the checkup form. We use it to run your checkup and reply to you. We do not sell it, and you can ask us to delete it at any time by emailing colesales@proton.me or calling 204-901-2941.

Who we are

YAVO builds and looks after websites and Google Business Profiles for local service businesses. We are based in Brandon, Manitoba and serve businesses across Canada. We follow the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA). For any privacy question or request, contact YAVO at colesales@proton.me or 204-901-2941.

What we collect

When you send the free checkup form we collect the details you provide:

  • Your name, phone number and email address
  • Your business name, city and province, and website address if you have one
  • What you need most, the example style you selected (if any), and anything you write in the details box

Our hosting provider may also record basic technical information such as IP address, browser type and pages viewed, which is standard for any website. We do not run advertising trackers or sell data to data brokers.

Why we collect it

We use this information only to prepare and send your checkup, reply to your request, provide a written quote if you ask for one, and keep records of work done for you. We contact you by the phone, text or email you gave us, and only about your checkup or the services you asked about.

Consent

The checkup form asks for your clear consent before anything is sent. You can withdraw consent at any time by contacting us. Withdrawing consent will not affect any work already completed.

Who can see it

Form messages are delivered through FlowForm, a form delivery service, to our business inbox at colesales@proton.me (hosted by Proton). The website is hosted on Vercel. These providers may process data outside Canada. We share personal information only with the service providers needed to run the website and deliver your request, or when required by law. We never sell or rent it.

How long we keep it

Checkup requests are kept while we prepare your checkup and follow up, then deleted within 12 months unless you become a client. Client records are kept for the period needed to provide the service and to meet legal and tax requirements.

Your choices and rights

You can ask what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, subject to legal requirements. Email colesales@proton.me and we will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Security

We use reputable providers, strong account passwords, two-factor authentication where available, and access limits to protect information. No online system is perfectly secure, so please do not send passwords, payment card numbers or other sensitive documents through the website form.

Example sites on this website

The example websites linked from our Example styles section use fictional business names and sample photos. Their quote forms are disabled and send nothing. If you fill one in, no information leaves your browser.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a material way, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
